# Cold Email Sequence — Automaly-Style Boutique Agencies

Targets: founders/partners at 5–30 person B2B automation, dev, and growth agencies (Automee, Brainpool, DevCommX + 17 similar). Send from your real address, plain text, no images, no tracking pixels. Personalize the `[bracketed]` lines — one specific observation per prospect, 2 minutes max each.

Cadence: Email 1 → Day +2: Email 2 → Day +4: Email 3 (breakup). Stop on any reply.

Positioning guardrails for all outreach: sell the outcome (a sharper pitch), name the product **Afilo PitchKit Growth Report**, describe it as a structured digital report — never as consulting, an audit service, or done-for-you marketing. Never promise sales, leads, or specific results.

## Rules

- Max 30 quality touches count toward the validation gate. A "touch" = Email 1 to a named founder at a fitting agency. Follow-ups don't count as new touches.
- If reply rate on Email 1 is <10% after 20 sends, rewrite the angle (Day 7 checkpoint) — change the email, never the price.
- Anyone who replies with a URL gets the free two-line angle within 12 hours. That reply IS the sales call.
- Never claim the report will generate sales, leads, or specific results — it sharpens the pitch; the deal is theirs to win.
