# LinkedIn DM + Warm-Network Messages

Positioning guardrails: the product is the **Afilo PitchKit Growth Report**, a software-assisted digital report being launched/validated now. Never frame it as consulting, an audit service, or done-for-you work. No guaranteed-results claims. Mention payment only as "one-time $149 via secure Paddle checkout" if it comes up.

---

## LinkedIn DM — cold, agency founders

Send after viewing their profile and one piece of their content. Two-message structure; never pitch in message 1 if they haven't accepted a connection yet.

**Connection request note (300 char limit):**

> Fellow agency operator (Afilo/TechSci, prev. CodeCraft). Liked your take on [specific post/case study]. Launching a digital report product for how boutique agencies pitch — thought you'd be the right person to sanity-check it.

**Message 1 (after accept, or as InMail):**

> Thanks for connecting, [First name]. Direct version: I've just launched the Afilo PitchKit Growth Report — you submit the URL of a client you're pitching plus a line of context, and within 48 hours you get a structured digital report: positioning diagnosis, stronger headline copy for their site, ranked conversion gaps, an outreach angle, and an SEO/content brief. $149 one-time.
>
> I'm validating it right now with agencies like yours. If you've got a live deal, send me the prospect's URL and I'll give you the opening angle I'd use — free, two lines, no strings. Worst case you stole a pitch idea from me.

**Message 2 (Day +3, no reply):**

> One-liner follow-up: sample insight from a recent report — "open with their pricing page, not their homepage; both competitors moved to usage pricing this year and they didn't." That's the level of specific. Sample report and checkout are at pitchkit.afilo.io if a pitch comes up this month.

Stop after message 2. No third touch on LinkedIn.

## Warm-network message — RoyalSoft Consulting / CodeCraft contacts

For past clients, collaborators, and agency friends. Send by whatever channel you normally talk on (WhatsApp/email/LinkedIn). Adjust the opener to the relationship — the ask stays identical.

> [First name] — Sayem here. Quick one, and it's a real ask, not a broadcast.
>
> I've launched a new product under Afilo: the PitchKit Growth Report. It's a software-assisted digital report — you submit the public URL of a client you're pitching plus a bit of context, and within 48 hours you get a structured growth and pitch report: positioning diagnosis, improved headline copy, conversion gaps, lead magnet ideas, an outreach angle, and an SEO/content brief. $149 one-time, paid through a secure Paddle checkout on the site.
>
> Two ways you could help, pick either or neither:
> 1. If you (or your team) have a client pitch coming up in the next couple of weeks, be one of my first buyers — I'll make sure the QA bar on yours is high.
> 2. If not, one intro to an agency founder who pitches B2B clients regularly would be worth a lot to me right now.
>
> Link if useful: pitchkit.afilo.io — happy to answer anything.

**Rules:** send to 5 contacts on Day 1, follow up once on Day 4 with a single line ("any pitch on your calendar this month?"). Warm buyers count toward the validation gate at full price — no friend discounts; the refund policy is the safety net.
